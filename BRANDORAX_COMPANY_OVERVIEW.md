# BRANDORAX
## Company Overview

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

BrandoraX is an online-first digital workforce development company designed to bridge the gap between skill acquisition and real-world employment. The company integrates structured tech training, real-time job placement systems, employer partnerships, and community empowerment initiatives into one scalable ecosystem.

Unlike traditional tech schools that stop at certification, BrandoraX focuses on measurable career outcomes by building a structured talent pipeline that connects trained individuals to startups, SMEs, and corporate organizations.

BrandoraX is not a conventional tech academy. It is a demand-driven talent pipeline that aligns training directly with real industry needs.

BrandoraX operates across three core pillars:

1. Academy (Skill acquisition and structured learning programs)
2. Talent Pipeline (facilitates internships, apprenticeships, and employment opportunities through employer partnerships.
3. Community Empowerment (Webinars, outreach programs, community development initiatives)

BrandoraX's objectives within its first two years is to train over 500 learners, achieve a 80-85% placement rate, and establish strong partnerships with at least 50 employer organizations.

## COMPANY DESCRIPTION

BrandoraX is a digital talent acceleration company established to solve a critical problem. Many individuals acquire skills but struggle to secure employment due to lack of practical experience and employer access.

BrandoraX addresses this gap through:

- Market-aligned training programs
- Structured project-based learning
- Employer-integrated placement systems
- Real-world project exposure
- Career readiness development

BrandoraX begins as an online-first company to maintain lean operational costs and scale efficiently. After achieving stable revenue and job placement success, the company plans to establish a physical innovation center.

## ACADEMY SECTOR

### BrandoraX Student-to-Employment Pipeline System

**Overview**

BrandoraX will operate a 7-stages Talent Pipeline that moves students from training into real employment opportunities.

Pipeline Flow:

Admission – Training – Evaluation – Elite – Selection – Talent pool – Employer matching – Job Deployment.

Only top-performing students (elite talent) will qualify for job placement opportunities through partner companies.

### STAGE 1: STUDENT ADMISSION SYSTEM

Students begin through the Brandora Academy.

**Admission Requirements**

Student must:

- Complete registration
- Choose a specialization
- Pass a basic assessment test (optional)
- Commit a program rule

**Available Specializations**

- UI/UX Design
- Graphic Design
- Web Dev
- Software Development
- Data Analysis
- Cybersecurity

Once admitted, students enter the training phase.

### STAGE 2: STRUCTURED TRAINING PHASE

**3 months Program**

- Foundational training
- Core skill development
- Mini projects

**6 months Program**

- Intermediate specialization
- Team project development
- Portfolio development

**12 months Program**

- Advanced specialization
- Industry level portfolio development
- Real-world problem solving

Students are required to complete:

- Skill assessments
- Project assignments/team collaboration projects
- Capstone projects

### STAGE 3: STUDENT PERFORMANCE EVALUATION

At the end of training, students are evaluated.

**Evaluation criteria:**

Students are assessed based on:

Technical skill
- Quality of work

Project quality
- How strong their final projects are

Professional discipline
- Meeting deadlines
- Communication skills
- Team collaboration

Problem solving

Portfolio strength

**Student ranking structure**

After evaluation, students are ranked into three categories.

**Tier 1: Developing talent**

Students are improving.

They receive:
- Certification
- Career guidance
- Portfolio review support

But no direct job placement access yet.

**Tier 2: Industry ready talent**

Students who meet industry standards.

They gain access to:
- Internship opportunities
- BrandoraX WorkLab projects

**Tier 3 – Elite talent (top 10-20%)**

These are the best-performing students.

They qualify for:
- Direct employer matching
- Partner company recruitment
- Premium job opportunities

This is where BrandoraX's job promise begins to activate.

### STAGE 5: BRANDORA ELITE TALENT POOL

Elite students enter the BrandoraX talent pool.

This is a curated list of top graduates presented to partner companies.

Each elite talent profile must contain:

- Portfolio
- Skill certification
- Performance ranking
- Mentor recommendation
- Work samples

This acts like a talent marketplace for employers.

### STAGE 6: EMPLOYER PARTNERSHIP NETWORK

BrandoraX will build partnerships with:

- Startups
- Tech companies
- Design agencies
- Digital marketing firms
- SaaS companies

These partners will:

- Recruit graduates
- Offer internships
- Hire remote workers
- Assign project contracts

**WHY COMPANIES WILL PARTNER WITH BRANDORAX**

Companies benefit because:

- Students are already trained
- Portfolios are verified
- Talent is pre-screened
- Hiring becomes easier

This saves them recruitment time and cost.

### STAGE 7: JOB MATCHING SYSTEM

When a partner company needs talent:

They submit a request to BrandoraX.

The BrandoraX Talent pool manager then:

- Reviews the request
- Searches the elite talent list pool
- Selects qualified candidates
- Sends profiles to the employer

### STAGE 8: CANDIDATE INTERVIEW PROCESS

The company then:

- Reviews candidate portfolios
- Shortlists candidates
- Conduct interviews

### STAGE 9: JOB DEPLOYMENT

If a student is selected:

They receive:

- Internship placement
- Remote work opportunity
- Full-time employment

BrandoraX records the placement in its Talent success database.

### STAGE 10: BRANDORAX PLACEMENT REVENUE MODEL

BrandoraX will generate revenue through:

Recruitment placement fees:
- Companies pay BrandoraX for successful hires.
- Training Partnerships:
- Companies sponsor talent development.

### STAGE 11: CONTINUOUS ALUMNI TRACKING

Graduates remain part of the BrandoraX Alumni Network

Benefits include:

- Advanced job opportunities
- Mentorship roles
- Leadership opportunities
- Startup collaborations

This strengthens the BrandoraX ecosystem long-term.
